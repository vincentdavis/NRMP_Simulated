Privacy

NRMP Simulations is a research and teaching tool. It keeps only what it needs to run your simulations and your account. Please do not upload personal data about real people.

What is stored

  • Your account: username, password (stored only as a salted hash), and the optional full name and email address you give. Email addresses are not verified yet.
  • Your simulations: their names and descriptions, configurations, the applicant and program populations you generate or upload, and the computed ratings and rankings.
  • Uploads: CSV files are read in memory and not kept as files; only the rows they contain are stored, as the simulation's population.
  • Failed sign-ins: to block password guessing, a failed sign-in records the username tried, the IP address and the browser identification. The record is cleared when that username signs in successfully. Successful sign-ins are not recorded.

Everything is stored in the site's database, hosted by the cloud provider Railway. Only you can see your simulations.

Logs and monitoring

To keep the site working, the server records technical information about requests: the page address, the time, the response status and error details. When enabled, these records are sent to the monitoring service Pydantic Logfire, which processes them on our behalf and keeps them according to its retention settings. Error messages identify records by number, not by the names in your data, and account fields such as email addresses are redacted.

Cookies

The site uses two cookies that it needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and a security (CSRF) cookie that protects forms. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a simulation deletes its configuration, populations and results. To delete your account and everything in it, {% if CONTACT_EMAIL %}email {{ CONTACT_EMAIL }}{% else %}contact the site operator through the issue tracker{% endif %}; self-service account deletion and data export are planned.

Your uploads

Use made-up or anonymised data. Do not upload information about real applicants or programs that identifies people, and do not upload data from the NRMP, ERAS or similar systems that you are not allowed to share.
