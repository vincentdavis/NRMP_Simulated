Before interviews

Agreement is the average correlation between two agents' true utilities (1 = everyone agrees). Fidelity is how well the pre-interview view matches the truth: the correlation of true and observed utilities over all pairs, and each agent's rank correlation between its true and its pre-interview ordering.
