What a run does

  • Population: applicants in groups, programs with positions
  • Before interviews: true preferences, seen with noise
  • Applications and signals: who applies where
  • Invitations: programs screen and invite in waves
  • Interviews: fit revealed, views sharpened
  • Rank order lists: each side ranks the other
  • Match: deferred acceptance, checked for stability
