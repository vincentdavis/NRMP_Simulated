Simulate the residency Match

Build a market of applicants and residency programs, give each side preferences and imperfect information, and see how their rankings form.

For medical students, program directors, educators and researchers. Results are simulations, not predictions of any real applicant's match.

Quick start

  1. Create an account (or log in) and open Simulations.
  2. Create a simulation. It starts with a working default configuration you can adjust.
  3. Generate applicants and programs, or upload your own as CSV.
  4. Initialize the interviews and compute the pre-interview ratings and rankings.
  5. Browse the results or download them as CSV.

Applications, invitations, interviews, rank order lists and the match itself are being built; see the project page.
